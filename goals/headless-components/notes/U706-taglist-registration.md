# Registering the taglist family

taglist merged with no way for a consumer to reach it and no lane that read it on a real page: the
barrel had no export, the package export map had no entry, the conformance battery had no
descriptor, the sr-gallery served no section, and none of the three CI reader matrices carried the
name. This wires all of that up. No taglist behaviour changed, and the four framework pins in
`taglist.browser.ts` are untouched and still expected-fail.

## What now carries `taglist`

- `src/index.ts` — `export * as taglist from './taglist/index.ts'`, between `tabs` and `textbox`.
- `package.json` — `"./taglist": "./src/taglist/index.ts"`, in the same slot.
- `test-support/conformance.browser.ts` — a `taglist` descriptor, between `tabs` and `textbox`.
- `api/manifest.json` — regenerated with `api:extract`.
- `apps/sr-gallery/preview-server.ts` — `taglist: '/#taglist'` in `FAMILY_ANCHORS`.
- `apps/sr-gallery/src/Gallery.tsrx` — a `#taglist` section carrying three shapes.
- `apps/sr-gallery/README.md` — regenerated by `scripts/anchor-table.ts`; 34 rows.
- `apps/sr-gallery/scripts/boot-check.ts` — a rendered role, a count, and four rows.
- `.github/workflows/screen-reader.yml` — the virtual, NVDA and VoiceOver matrices.

All eight scenarios moved from `import * as taglist from '../index.ts'` to the consumer form
`import { taglist } from '../../index.ts'`, and the three-line comment each carried explaining why
they could not is gone. `taglist-transcript.ts` reads `FAMILY_ANCHORS.taglist` instead of exporting
a `TAGLIST_ANCHOR` literal, and takes `GALLERY_WALK_LIMIT` instead of a per-file `WALK_LIMIT = 200`;
both reader lanes import the anchor from `preview-server.ts` rather than from the transcript.

## The manifest was one doc line stale, not silent about taglist

The extractor already named this family — `api/manifest.json` at the pilot tip carried a `taglist`
key with every part, because it walks the folders under `src/` rather than the barrel. So adding the
barrel export changed nothing structural. What `api:extract` did change is one line: the `editable`
prop's doc on `taglist.root` still read the pre-F2 wording ("Enter on a highlighted tag, or a
double-click"), while `taglist-types.ts` has said "Enter on a highlighted tag while the caret is in
the field, F2 on a tag that has focus, or a double-click" since the family landed. The manifest is
regenerated with that sentence. `api:check` passes (3 rows).

## The descriptor: a group with no collection role, and no open cycle

`rootAria` is `{ role: 'group', 'aria-disabled': null }`. The `null` is the fact that this root
writes no `aria-disabled` at all — it reports disablement through `ui-disabled` and through the
input's own `disabled`. `datebox` writes `'false'` in that slot and `editable` writes nothing; the
descriptor records which.

There is no `openCycle`. Nothing here opens: the row has no surface, and the per-tag edit field is
`hidden` in the same room rather than something a trigger expands, so the six rows an `openCycle`
adds are absent rather than exempted.

`parts` is `['root', 'label', 'input', 'field']`. The chips are the consumer's own markup keyed by
each tag's words, so their testids read `item-alpha`, `itemlabel-alpha`, `itemclose-alpha` — values,
not fixed part names, and the battery resolves parts by exact `data-testid`. The presentational
`row` div the repeat opens in is the scenario's own markup and is not a part.

`valuedAttributes` is `['ui-value']`, from the battery run rather than from reading the source: the
spelling row went green with that one name. `ui-disabled`, `ui-required`, `ui-invalid`, `ui-empty`
on the root, `ui-empty` on the input and `ui-highlighted`/`ui-editing` on an item are all presence
marks. `ui-value` is on the item, its close button and its edit input — the tag's own words are the
identity every part inside a chip is keyed by, which is why it is key-value.

12 conformance rows, 6 per mode, no exemptions.

## Where the section sits

`#taglist` is served immediately after `#editable`, which is itself after `#buttongroup`. A taglist
root is a `role="group"`, and per U703 NVDA says "grouping" for a `radiogroup` too, so a section of
groups placed before `#radio-group` would end that family's still-role-only walk on a taglist. Put
after `#editable` it joins the run of group-serving sections (`crop`, `crop-image`, `editable`) that
already sit below every role-only walk on the page.

## The gallery serves three shapes

The tags input (`name="topics"`, label "Topics", `alpha`/`beta`, with `taglist.field`), the
display-only filter row (label "Active filters", `red`/`green`/`blue`, no `taglist.input` at all)
and the editable one (label "Stages", `draft`/`review`, each item mounting `taglist.iteminput`).
Each is a small component of the page's own — `TopicTags`, `FilterTags`, `StageTags` — because the
chips are consumer markup repeated over a consumer array, and that array has to live on a state
object inside a component rather than in the one `Gallery()` body.

`RENDERED_ROLE.taglist` is `group`: the root is the only element a shape puts in the accessibility
tree whether or not it mounts a field, since the chips carry no role. `RENDERED_COUNT.taglist` of 3
then catches a section that rendered the starter and lost a shape.

## Four boot-check rows, none of which mutate the page

The role count says three shapes rendered; what a reader lane then turns on is that a row with no
collection role is still navigable. The group is named by its label part through `aria-labelledby`
("Topics"), each of `Remove alpha` and `Remove beta` is exactly one button — the tag's own words
reaching a person through the control that removes it, which is the whole semantics decision — and
three `output[aria-live="polite"]` regions are mounted, one per shape. Nothing here clicks or types,
so unlike the `editable` block these rows sit above it rather than at the end of the file.

## Verification

`pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm typecheck:sr` and `pnpm typecheck:sr-real` are clean.
`pnpm --filter @markless/ui api:check` — 3 rows passed.
`pnpm exec vitest run --project ui packages/headless/components/src/taglist
packages/headless/components/test-support/conformance.browser.ts -t "taglist"` — 16 passed.
The full `taglist.browser.ts` suite was also run without the name filter, because the filter would
have hidden a scenario-import regression across the eight files that changed: 91 passed, 4 expected
fail. At the pilot tip the same unfiltered run was 90 passed, 1 failed, 4 expected fail — the
first-gesture flake the packet named; it did not reappear on either run after the change.
`pnpm test:sr` — 40 files, 315 passed, 10 expected fail, 4 skipped: identical to the tip.
`node apps/sr-gallery/scripts/boot-check.ts` — green, with the five new lines:

```
#taglist serves the taglist family: 3 role="group" element(s)
#taglist serves a group named by its label part.
#taglist names its delete button "Remove alpha".
#taglist names its delete button "Remove beta".
#taglist mounts the live region that speaks a change, one per shape.
```

The boot check and the walk measurement ran on `SR_GALLERY_PORT=4341`, not the 4337 the packet
named: another worktree was already answering on 4337 and the squatter guard is there precisely so a
check does not read someone else's tree. Nothing was killed.

The two real-reader matrices were added on the evidence that `taglist.nvda.ts` and
`taglist.voiceover.ts` exist, which is the rule those lists state. Neither reader lane was run here:
real-reader lanes need a VM or a CI runner, not a desktop.

U703 measured a full pass at 435 announcements with `#drawer`'s trigger at 430; the drawer heading
now sits at 472, so the taglist section costs about 43 and a pass is roughly 480.
`GALLERY_WALK_LIMIT` is 900 — no longer quite twice a pass, but still comfortably more than the one
full pass a walk needs from any starting point, so no limit was raised. The next family to land on
this page should re-measure rather than assume the margin holds.

## Still open

- `radio-group-transcript.ts` walks to `{ role: 'radiogroup' }` and `modal-transcript.ts` to
  `{ role: 'dialog' }`, both by role alone. Both are live units and untouched, and both are correct
  only because of where their sections sit — which is the constraint that decided where `#taglist`
  went. Until they match on a name, section order on this page is load-bearing.
- The taglist section is the sixth on the page to serve a `role="group"` root. The virtual and real
  readers can only tell them apart by name, and every one of those sections currently names its
  root; nothing enforces that.
- The four framework pins in `taglist.browser.ts` (hidden inputs never minted for a tag the first
  render did not carry, and a consumer component's text over the collection not refreshing) are
  still red-by-design. Registration does not touch them; they are runtime work.
