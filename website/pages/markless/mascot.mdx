A warm little companion for whatever you’re making. Pick a moment, try on a face, and dress the mug for a coffee break.

Twelve little coffee rituals, sixteen expressions, seven glazes, and four latte pours. Early motion exploration drew on
[bloub](https://bloub.vercel.app/), Jérémy Perret’s [MIT-licensed study](https://github.com/jeremy-prt/bloub).
The ceramic character, coffee-bean eyes, and coffee-break rituals are drawn for Markless.
